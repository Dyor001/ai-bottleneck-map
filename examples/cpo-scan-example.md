# Example: CPO Theme Scan

## Opening judgment

Start with the layers, not the obvious leaders.

For CPO, the higher-priority research path is often:

1. optical alignment and coupling
2. optical test and yield-sensitive packaging
3. laser and substrate-adjacent enabling layers

The reason is simple: when demand ramps, the first bottlenecks are often not the most famous brands but the layers that are hardest to qualify, scale, or dual-source.

## Example answer shape

### Layer priority

- FAU and alignment-sensitive optical coupling
- PMLA and other precision optical elements
- optical foundry and test throughput

### Candidate mapping

- Company A: direct exposure to the constrained layer, strong if customer evidence confirms qualification
- Company B: clean supplier mapping, but timing depends on pilot-to-ramp conversion
- Company C: interesting edge case, but current mapping is still inferential

### What would upgrade the thesis

- customer qualification confirmation
- higher-volume production references
- capex or capacity expansion tied to the layer

### What would weaken the thesis

- the company only appears in sampling, not production
- the relevant revenue is too small to matter
- a larger competitor can scale faster and remove scarcity
